# iraq-ecommerce-research

Version: 0.3.0

Gather dated primary or clearly classified local evidence. Compare Erbil, Sulaymaniyah, Duhok and Baghdad only where sources support coverage. Separate verified facts, estimates and assumptions. Verify exact supplier shipping and carrier/COD terms. Record merchant acceptance, settlement, eligibility, withdrawal and cross-border transfer separately. Produce the complete opportunity contract in templates/ecommerce-opportunity.md. Use existing income skills for demand, fraud checks, payout-chain validation and lean experiments. No purchases, outreach, accounts, spending or publishing is implied.
