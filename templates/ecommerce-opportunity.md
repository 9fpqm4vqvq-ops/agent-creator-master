# Iraq e-commerce opportunity — unverified draft

State VERIFIED, ESTIMATE or ASSUMPTION for every externally checkable claim, with
source URL, checked timestamp, confidence, applicability and contradictions. Blank
fields are missing evidence; they are not permission to invent observations.

## Business model and target customers

Business model; product category; target customer segment; initial cities/region;
problem addressed; selling channel access; evidence of demand and competition.

## Supplier feasibility

Exact supplier/product; provenance/authenticity; MOQ and inventory ownership;
local versus imported options; verified Iraq shipping/merchant terms; lead times;
quality and return responsibilities; evidence gaps and alternative suppliers.

## Fulfillment approach

Coverage and quotes for Erbil, Sulaymaniyah, Duhok, Baghdad or other scoped markets;
outbound shipping; failed delivery and return-to-origin costs; customs/cross-border
constraints; lead times; COD service and settlement timing; inventory handling.

## Estimated costs and margins under explicit assumptions

Startup capital; unit costs; packaging; advertising per attempted order; cancellation,
delivery and return assumptions; retained sales, revenue, contribution, breakeven;
fees/taxes/customs; inventory recovery assumptions; sensitivity; working capital.
No actual income or sales volume is implied by a hypothetical scenario.

## Payment feasibility

For each provider considered (for example ZainCash, Qi Card, FastPay or banking/cards),
verify separately: customer payment acceptance, merchant eligibility, cleared funds,
settlement destination/timing, withdrawals and international transfers. For COD,
verify carrier collection, settlement, fees, account eligibility and usable funds.
Do not infer merchant onboarding or cross-border access from a consumer product page.

## Legal and operational requirements

Current authoritative Iraqi/Kurdistan Region business setup and product rules;
merchant eligibility; product restrictions; tax/customs uncertainties; professional
questions requiring confirmation. Record facts and uncertainties separately.

## Key risks and evidence confidence

Demand, supplier, fulfillment, returns, cash flow, authenticity, platform access,
legal constraints and payments; source freshness; confidence and unresolved gaps.

## Suggested validation experiment

Hypothesis; smallest lawful test; exact metric/threshold; maximum cost/hours;
stop conditions; required permissions; evidence needed before external execution.
No purchases, ads, messages, accounts or publication without actual authorization.

performed; reviewer invocation reference; PASS/REWORK/BLOCKED; precise corrections.
